# /novel.expand

`/novel.expand` is deprecated.

Use `/novel.realize` instead.

`Realize` replaced `Expand` because the step is not just about adding words. It may deepen conflict, sharpen voice, replace summary with enacted scene work, and trigger reconciliation when better canon emerges.

If the author invokes `/novel.expand`, redirect immediately to `/novel.realize` and preserve the same novel context.
